# Bachata full-content perceptual audit

Coverage is **319/319 Bachata lessons** across **16 course folders**. Every non-Carolina lesson received a direct source title/identity inspection plus a separate content-frame inspection; the 30 Carolina lessons carry forward the prior direct Bunny-title-card and local-duration reconciliation.

## Result

- Confirmed lesson/course identities: **304**
- Source/course-label mismatches: **15** (all 15 are the Pablo & Raquel folder labeled Intermediate whose cards say Intermediate/Advanced)
- Ambiguous identities: **0**
- Category confirmed as Bachata: **319/319**
- Wrong-dance or cross-course asset swaps found: **0**
- Direct title cards transcribed: **318/319**; one Korke/Judith 2025 introduction has no discrete card but is otherwise directly identified
- Display-title differences: **29 material**, **54 source-fidelity**, **236 none**

The user-reported Carolina issue was a category defect, not a swapped video: all 30 Carolina lessons are source-confirmed Traditional Bachata, and the current hardening worktree now assigns them to Bachata. No Bachata lesson inspected in this 319-video scope was actually a Salsa video or a different instructor/course asset.

## Method and limits

This was a bounded perceptual audit: identifying/title frames and independent content frames were inspected, with denser two-second scans and post-logo follow-ups when the first candidate missed a card. It was not an end-to-end watch of every minute. `identityStatus: mismatch` here means the source level disagrees with the assigned course label; it does not mean a wrong dance video. A null `observedTitle` is used only for the 2025 introduction, where no discrete title card appeared but instructor, spoken introduction, course setting, and Bachata content confirm identity.

Canonical per-lesson fields, evidence timestamps/methods/details, confidence, issues, and display recommendations are in [bachata_remaining.json](./bachata_remaining.json). Source-confirmed course-label recommendations are separately in [bachata_course_labels.json](./bachata_course_labels.json).
