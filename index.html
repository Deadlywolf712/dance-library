Guide

▶ Video Player

  • Speed control — Click the speed button (top-right of video) or use the Learn/Practice/Full presets below the player.
  • Mirror mode — Flip the video horizontally so you can follow along as if looking in a mirror. Press M or click the mirror button.
  • Skip buttons — Jump 5 seconds forward/back. Use the buttons or arrow keys.
  • A-B Loop — Set a start point (A) and end point (B) to loop a section endlessly. Click the A button or press [ then ]. Press Esc to clear.
  • Resume playback — Your position is saved automatically. Come back to any video and it picks up where you left off.
  • Theater mode — Press T or click the expand button to go fullscreen with a dark backdrop. Press Esc to exit.

★ Bookmarks & Notes

  • Add a bookmark — Click "+ Add" below the video to bookmark the current timestamp.
  • Add notes — After adding a bookmark, type a note (optional). You can also click the pencil icon on any bookmark to edit its note.
  • Click a bookmark — Jumps to that exact timestamp in the video.
  • Favorites — Click the star next to the video title to favorite it. Favorites appear at the top of your home page.

📖 Notes Manager

  • Click the notebook icon in the sidebar to see all your bookmarks, notes, and favorites in one place.
  • Search — Filter notes by typing in the search bar.
  • Click any note — Loads the video and jumps to that exact timestamp.
  • Edit/Delete — Use the pencil and × buttons to modify or remove entries.
  • Filter — Toggle between "All" bookmarks or "With notes" only.

🔎 Navigation

  • Sidebar search — Type in the search bar to filter the video library.
  • Spotlight search — Press Ctrl+K (or ⌘+K) for a fast floating search. Hit Enter to play.
  • Breadcrumbs — Click any part of the breadcrumb trail to jump back to that folder level.
  • Continue Watching — Videos you haven't finished appear on the home page with your saved position.
  • Previous/Next — Navigate between videos in the same folder.

🎨 Themes & Settings

  • Click the gear icon to open Settings. Choose from 30+ color themes.
  • Configure your Bunny CDN pull zone for faster streaming.
  • Reset watch history, bookmarks, favorites, or everything from Settings.

⌨ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Press ? anytime to see all keyboard shortcuts.
